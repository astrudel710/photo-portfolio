About Me

The eye
behind the lens

I'm a photographer driven by curiosity — from the sweeping volcanic plateaus of Iceland to the intimate world of a beetle on a single leaf, I look for moments where light, form, and meaning quietly align.

My work sits at the intersection of landscape and nature photography, with a tendency toward the abstract and experimental. I believe the most compelling images live in the quiet details others walk past — a shadow, a reflection, an iridescent wing.

Based in the San Francisco Bay Area. Available for commissions, editorial work, and fine art prints worldwide.
